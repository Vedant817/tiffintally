## E. Evidence kept deliberately open

- Train and legacy test are synthetic with template-authored labels; the test split
  shares sibling templates with train. Documented in `docs/KNOWN_LEAKS.md`; Wilson
  intervals do not correct for that.
- Historical model figures were retracted because the per-row outputs were never
  saved and the adapter cannot be re-scored. The retraction and its reason are in
  `evals/retracted/`, `docs/NUMBERS.md` and `RESULTS.md`.
- `tests/test_split_frozen.py` pins the frozen split. This work does not modify it.
- Human holdout labelling must be blind, independently checked, and split before any
  scoring. The loader requires collector, date, consent, source, labeller,
  `label_date` and `second_labeller` on every row. A repeated call must not consume a
  previously scored holdout to tune rules.
  Use `scripts/label_cli.py label --input data/private/raw_real.txt --provenance
  data/private/provenance.json` to author all events without model suggestions.
  Independently review labels, then use its `freeze` command before an explicit
  private `--human-holdout` run. Complete the blind repeat-label agreement check
  described in `docs/planning/FIX_PROMPT4.md`; the agent cannot supply that evidence.
- The new dev2 rows are agent-authored synthetic development data, not human labels.
  Review the proposed secondary-allocation errata before using it as training gold.
- Raw customer exports belong under the ignored path `data/private/`, never in the
  repository.

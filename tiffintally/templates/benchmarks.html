What this page is and is not

Scorer demo. Needs gold labels. Starts from the offline rules (or an injected fake flaw), applies hand-written repair rules written for these four sentences, and is not used when you paste a chat. The adversarial suite is expected to fail.

No model is called from this page. A high score here means the repair rules recognise the sentence they were written for, not that extraction is accurate on messages they have not seen.
